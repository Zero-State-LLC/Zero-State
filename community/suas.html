What it is

SUAS (“Shut up and serve.”) is civic work built by zer0state. It is the zer0state entry for the Veteran Innovation Hackathon at Hacker Dojo, 28–30 August 2026.

When a veteran needs help, finding it should not be another battle.

Zero State lists SUAS as community work. SUAS is not a Zero State product. It is not listed for sale.

The Los Altos Town Crier covered the Hacker Dojo weekend on 23 August 2026 PT in a piece about Silver’s SUAS QRF and the first Hackathons for good event; the article does not name zer0state.
