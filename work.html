04
Tactical cyberpunk RPG

HexWire

HexWire is a tactical cyberpunk hex-grid RPG for iPhone and iPad built in SwiftUI and SpriteKit. Its pressure-driven combat loop makes every action trade power for exposure: Signal → Power → Trace → Escalation → Lay Low → Tempo Tradeoff.

Four runners—street samurai, mage, decker, and face—move through six authored multi-room missions and interstitial scenes, supported by a persistent economy, faction heat, seeded replay rerolls, an endless gauntlet, and New Game+ scaling. GameState remains the single gameplay authority while UI and SpriteKit layers emit intents and render from state.

Stabilized vertical slice · CI-verified · device and App Store release gates remain

05
Satirical survivor roguelite

Surveillance Survivor

Surveillance Survivor is an iPhone-first satirical survivor roguelite about dodging privatized cameras, weaponizing suspicion, and extracting through temporary Blind Spots across a ten-city American campaign.

Its deterministic Swift core is authoritative while SpriteKit projects the gameplay state through a SwiftUI application shell. The current vertical slice includes escalating Suspicion, destructible LPR poles, upgrades, district authorities, campaign unlocks, and Blind Spot extraction.

Active pre-alpha · simulator-ready vertical slice · not release-ready
