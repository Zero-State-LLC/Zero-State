About

A software company at the beginning of its work.

Zero State develops software from an adaptive, first-principles philosophy.

Our position

We are neither waiting for the future nor claiming to own it. We are learning how to build responsibly within changing technological conditions.

Our method

We begin from reality, remove expired assumptions, preserve what remains true, and use emerging technologies deliberately.

Our standard

We allow the quality, coherence, and durability of the work to reveal whether the philosophy has value.

Public sources

Product work is published under the Zero-State-LLC organization. Portfolio pages link to each repository as the current public source of truth. App Store and production product URLs are not claimed until a release is ready.
