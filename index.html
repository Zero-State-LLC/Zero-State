Local intelligence.

Local intelligence on your own hardware.

Private AI that runs on hardware you control, such as NVIDIA GPUs. We set up models, agents, and retrieval inside your walls, designed so the work gets sharper and the data stays home.

Talk about a deployment →

  • Private knowledge

    Ask across your own documents, tickets, and records. Designed so answers cite their sources and the data stays on your network.

  • Agents at work

    Agents designed to work inside your tools, with their actions logged for review.

  • Voice and media

    Transcription, speech, and media pipelines designed to keep recordings on your machines.

  • Plain-language limits

    Each deployment is designed to include tests, receipts, and a clear statement of what it will not do.
