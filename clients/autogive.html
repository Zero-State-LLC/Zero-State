What it is

Autogive.app is the public product of Autonomously Giving Incorporated. Follow a contribution from funding intent to verified community impact—with the evidence needed to understand what happened next.

Zero State lists Autogive as client work. Autogive is not a Zero State product. Zero State does not process donations.

In short

The live site presents an inspectable contribution journey: funding intent, allocation, verification, and community impact. Gift tracking is third-party. Autogive does not process donations.

Public signals on the site use bundled deterministic fixtures, not live donor data. The current page is demonstration evidence, not a live payment or one-to-one attribution system.
