# Design — Zero State

A locked design system for the Zero State public website.

## Genre

Modern-minimal: quiet, structural, and exact. The Balanced Aperture mark and
its highway-centerline transition are the only expressive motion.

## Macrostructure family

- Marketing pages: Marquee Hero with asymmetric editorial sections.
- Product pages: Workbench product brief.
- Content and legal pages: Long Document.

## Theme

- Paper: `#FBF9F4`
- Stone: `#F4F0E8`
- Ink: `#1D2321`
- Rule: `#B9B2A7`
- Accent: `#F2C200`, used only as a signal.
- Secondary accent: `#486F6A`.

## Typography

- Display: Georgia, normal style, 600–700.
- Body: Inter/system sans, 400–700.
- Mono: system monospace, labels and metadata only.

## Spacing and motion

Use the named 4-point tokens in `styles.css`. The motion stance is quiet:
no decorative reveals, no hover transforms, and one linear highway pass with a
reduced-motion off switch.

## Shared page rules

- A masthead replaces generic sticky SaaS navigation.
- Section labels appear only where they name a real category or product domain.
- Footer closes with the company statement rather than a sitemap.
- Product names and the Balanced Aperture mark remain unchanged.
