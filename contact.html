Primary support and contact address. We read every message.

How to get a fast reply

  • App support: include the app name (for example Marigold Market), device model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened.
  • Purchases / restores: include the approximate purchase date and whether you are signed into the same Apple ID used for the purchase. We process refunds through Apple when the platform requires it.
  • Privacy requests: put “Privacy Request” in the subject line. See our Privacy Policy for rights and timelines.
  • Partnerships & press: introduce the work and the problem clearly; include a deadline if you have one.

  • Accessibility feedback
