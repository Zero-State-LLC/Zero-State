Neon Genie

Turn weak signals into evidence-bound plans and packets — without inventing buyers, capital, or proof.

v3.36.0 · advisory only

What it is

Product and opportunity intelligence for Hermes: audits, constrained roadmaps, commercial models, and handoffs with labeled claims.

Public facts can be researched; private facts are requested, never invented. Advice only — no spend, publish, contact, or repo mutation.

In short

Start with a short packaging wizard when you are new; then work the problem in chat with OPEN → SEAL discipline.
