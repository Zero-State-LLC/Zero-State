What it is

HexWire is a tactical cyberpunk hex-grid RPG for iPhone and iPad.

Power costs exposure. Four runner roles, authored missions, and a pressure-driven combat loop.

Signal → Power → Trace → Escalation → Lay Low → Tempo Tradeoff.

In short

Tactical cyberpunk hex-grid RPG for iPhone and iPad. Power costs exposure; four runner roles, authored missions, and a pressure-driven combat loop.

The campaign includes a persistent economy with nuyen, black-market access, and cyberware; faction heat between missions; seeded replay rerolls; an endless gauntlet; and New Game+ scaling.

GameState is the single gameplay authority. UI and SpriteKit layers emit intents and render from state. Owner-device touch-layer checks, disabling development mission unlock, and App Store signing remain release blockers.
