What it is

HexWire is a tactical cyberpunk hex-grid RPG for iPhone and iPad, built with SwiftUI and SpriteKit.

Its pressure-driven combat loop is Signal → Power → Trace → Escalation → Lay Low → Tempo Tradeoff. Four runners—street samurai, mage, decker, and face—operate across six authored multi-room missions and interstitial scene-missions.

Systems and architecture

The campaign includes a persistent economy with nuyen, black-market access, and cyberware; faction heat that persists between missions; seeded replay rerolls; an endless gauntlet; and New Game+ scaling.

GameState is the single gameplay authority. The UI and SpriteKit layers emit intents and render from state rather than mutating authoritative state directly.

The current repository posture is a stabilized vertical slice with deterministic tests and CI-verified iPhone and iPad builds. Owner-device touch-layer checks, disabling the development mission unlock, and App Store signing and metadata remain release blockers.
