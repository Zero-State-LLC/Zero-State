Hybrid-casual sorting puzzle

Marigold Market

Cozy ASMR shelf-sorting for iPhone. Match triples, restock rooms, and clear levels without timers or lives.

Pre-release 0.1.x · iOS

What it is

Marigold Market is a hybrid-casual Goods Sort style experience: shelves, triples, back rows, and modifiers introduced gently. Failure never erases progress—it tempts a rescue. Every level is designed to feel good in a fifteen-second capture.

Juice is the product: haptics, foley, and pop are core mechanics, not polish bolted on later. Rules, generator, and solver live in a UI-free GameCore package with a large unit test suite.

Sets and platform

Item sets in flight include Pantry, Café, Craft room, and Vanity. Stack is SwiftUI, SpriteKit, pure-Swift GameCore, and XcodeGen. Home-screen label: Marigold.
