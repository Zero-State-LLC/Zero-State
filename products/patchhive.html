Eurorack rig intelligence

PatchHive

A deterministic Eurorack rig-intelligence and patch-book publishing system — without synthesizing audio or controlling hardware.

v0.3.0-alpha · not production · payments test-mode only

What it is

PatchHive converts a verified modular rig into immutable, explainable patch libraries and publication-ready exports. The publishing invariant: every published patch is complete, executable, and understandable on exactly one standalone page.

It is a compiler and publishing system—not a preset marketplace, audio simulator, DSP engine, or hardware-control surface.

Current posture

Canonical MVP and PatchBook Design Engine land on main; design engine flags default off. Visual System Intelligence P0 covers photo evidence, inventory gate, and multi-photo fusion with mock vision. Production deploy has not been performed; live payments stay disabled.
