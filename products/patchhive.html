PatchHive

A deterministic Eurorack rig-intelligence and patch-book publishing system — without synthesizing audio or controlling hardware.

What it is

PatchHive turns a verified modular rig into clear, publishable patch libraries.

Every published patch is complete and readable on a single page — a compiler and publishing system, not a synth or hardware controller.

In short

Turn a verified modular rig into clear, publishable patch books — complete on one page, without pretending to be a synth or hardware controller.
