What it is

Abraxas v2.0 is not a generic chatbot wrapper. It is an implementation-first architecture for traceable symbolic intelligence: deterministic execution paths, artifact lineage and receipts, audit surfaces, recursive continuity under frozen governance rules, contract-bound operators, and lane-based activation.

Its default posture is advisory and projection-only for research and operator surfaces unless a governed lane explicitly authorizes more. Candidate status does not mean production activation, and repository merges do not imply go-live.
