# Intent

## Problem

The home page's three marks (the hero aperture mark, the method glyph, and the
local-intelligence enclosure) are flat images. The owner wants them presented as 3D
objects in the same style, so the home page shows the brand's craft rather than
describing it.

## Proposed outcome

- The hero mark, the method glyph and the enclosure are drawn in 3D (WebGL) in exactly
  the position and size of the current flat art. The flat art stays in the page as the
  fallback when WebGL is unavailable, and it still holds the layout.
- The hero PNG's wordmark strip still shows, so the wordmark is unchanged.
- Each mark turns when dragged and eases back to face front.
- The hero mark assembles from its layers once on load. The method passage keeps its
  brand-token motion (one 4800 ms linear pass on reveal). In the enclosure the ember
  dot travels between Models, Agents and Your data, and the dotted ring turns slowly.
- The hero orbit rings also turn slowly at rest; the scroll-linked turn is unchanged.
- `design.md` records the new motion.

Out of scope: inner pages, the nav mark, the static brand assets, copy.

## Affected users / systems

- Home page visitors (`index.html`, `home.css`).
- New `aperture-3d.js` and `marks-3d.js`.
- `design.md` motion section.

## Constraints / non-goals

- This changes the identity motion, so it needs explicit brand review before merge.
- three.js (MIT) loads from jsDelivr at a pinned version (0.170.0). No other
  third-party code or assets.
- `prefers-reduced-motion`: no assembly, sway, travelling dot, ring turn or passage
  motion; dragging still works.
- No WebGL: nothing changes from today's page.
- `npm test` passes unchanged.

## Open questions

1. Brand review: does Danny accept the 3D treatment and the new idle motion?
2. Should three.js be self-hosted under `assets/` instead of loaded from jsDelivr?
   Proposed: CDN for now. Decides: owner.
