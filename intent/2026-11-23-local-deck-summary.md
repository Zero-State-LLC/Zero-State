# Intent

## Problem

The homepage `#local` section and `local-intelligence.html` describe the private-AI service, but a visitor can't see what Zero State actually pitches to clients: the problem, how it works, what it costs to start, and the proof. That lives only in the private "Zero State Private AI" client deck.

## Proposed outcome

A "See the pitch deck in two minutes" button in the homepage `#local` section opens a full-screen slide player with a 13-slide summary of the client deck. `local-intelligence.html` links to it with `index.html#deck`.

Out of scope: the full deck, speaker notes, client names, and per-package prices beyond the starting price.

## Affected users / systems

Site visitors on the homepage and the Local intelligence page. `index.html`, `home.css`, `home.js`, `local-intelligence.html`. `workers/ask/knowledge.md` is regenerated by `scripts/ask-knowledge.js` because the homepage text changed.

## Constraints / non-goals

- Copy comes only from the owner-approved client deck; nothing new is invented.
- This departs on purpose from the 2026-11-22 AEO intent's "no prices, metrics or customers" constraint. It adds the starting price, first-pass document-reader accuracy with its caveat, and an unnamed mortgage-lender build. The co-founder should accept or strike those three in review.
- Hardware stays generic ("sized to the work"), matching the Local intelligence page's rule against naming a default GPU model.
- No person is named.

## Open questions

- Keep the starting price public? (Jev: show it, confidence 0.24.)
- Keep the accuracy figures public? (Jev: show them with the caveat, confidence 0.80.)

- Format: Jev preferred an inline expander (0.38). The owner asked for something you click that brings up a summary, and then for "whatever looks most impressive", so this uses a full-screen player.
