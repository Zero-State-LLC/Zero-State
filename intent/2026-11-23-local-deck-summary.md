# Intent

## Problem

The homepage `#local` section and `local-intelligence.html` describe the private-AI service, but a visitor can't see what Zero State actually pitches to clients: the problem, how it works, what it costs to start, and the proof. That lives only in the private "Zero State Private AI" client deck.

## Proposed outcome

A "Take the two-minute tour" button in the homepage `#local` section opens a full-screen slide player with a 13-slide summary of the client deck. `local-intelligence.html` links to it with `index.html#tour`. Visible copy never says "pitch deck" (owner's call).

Out of scope: the full deck, speaker notes, client names, and per-package prices beyond the starting price.

## Affected users / systems

Site visitors on the homepage and the Local intelligence page. `index.html`, `home.css`, `home.js`, `local-intelligence.html`. `workers/ask/knowledge.md` is regenerated by `scripts/ask-knowledge.js` because the homepage text changed.

## Constraints / non-goals

- Copy comes only from the owner-approved client deck; nothing new is invented.
- **Resolved 2026-11-23 (review):** the 2026-11-22 AEO intent's "no prices, metrics or customers" constraint stands. The starting price, the first-pass accuracy figures, and the unnamed mortgage-lender reference are struck from the public deck. The Evidence and Packages slides now describe the audit and pricing process qualitatively (fixed price per stage, hardware at cost, every figure cites its source) with no dollar amount, percentage, document count, or client/industry reference.
- Hardware stays generic ("sized to the work"), matching the Local intelligence page's rule against naming a default GPU model.
- No person is named.
